# PHONELAB SUITE — Brand Charter

## Positioning

PHONELAB SUITE is a family of small, focused mobile tools built around practical phone capabilities: sensors, sound, image, timing, communication and experimentation.

The suite is a family identity, not a monolithic application.

## Brand architecture

PHONELAB SUITE
→ individual product name
→ individual accent / icon
→ shared design language

Every product must remain independently understandable and independently releasable.

## Visual principles

1. Mobile first.
2. Clear before decorative.
3. High contrast in both light and dark themes.
4. One primary action per view where possible.
5. Product-specific accent, shared structural language.
6. No fake sensor readings or misleading visual affordances.
7. Accessibility is part of the visual identity.

Public trademark/name availability is not assumed and must be checked before store naming is frozen.
